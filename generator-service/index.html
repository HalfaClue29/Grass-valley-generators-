Generator service and maintenance in Grass Valley and Nevada County

Every installed standby generator needs yearly maintenance if it is going to start during a shutoff. This page explains what that visit usually includes. A licensed installer performs the work. This site is a lead service that connects you with that installer.

Annual maintenance

Once a year, a standby unit should get oil, filters, plugs, a battery check and a full test run under load, on a schedule instead of after a failure. That visit is what decides whether the unit starts during the outage that matters.

When it does not start

A standby unit that fails its weekly self-test is telling you something. Service here covers the brands homeowners in this market actually have installed, Generac first among them, with Kohler and Cummins units also present.

Yearly service plans

A yearly plan turns maintenance into a standing appointment. Plan pricing is set by the servicing installer. This page does not list a plan price.
