Whole-house generators for Lake Wildwood, California

Lake Wildwood is a gated community where a lot of owners are retired or part-time residents. For a house that sits empty for stretches, a standby unit with a yearly service plan is protection that does not need anyone home to start it.

What matters most here

Automatic startup, remote monitoring so an empty house still reports in, and maintenance handled on schedule whether the owner is in town or not. Yearly service — oil, filters, plugs, a battery check and a test run under load — is what keeps a unit ready.
