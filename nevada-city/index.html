Grass Valley Generators / Nevada City

Whole-house generators for Nevada City, California

Nevada City is three miles from Grass Valley. This page is for homeowners there who want a standby generator sized for a historic-town lot, older electrical work, and PG&E shutoffs in Nevada County.

The 2020 census counted 3,152 people in Nevada City, against 14,016 in Grass Valley. Homes here are often smaller and older, on tighter lots, so placement, clearance and the electrical panel get a close look at a free in-home estimate. A licensed installer does that visit and any later installation or service. This site is a lead service that makes that introduction. It is not the installer.

What a generator costs and how long it takes

The leading Generac dealer serving this area publishes an average installed cost of $13,000 to $16,000. Size, fuel run, panel condition and placement move a real quote around that figure. The same dealer’s published install wait is 8 to 10 weeks from prepayment. This page does not promise a shorter wait.

Cost and wait figures as published by the leading local Generac dealer, November 2026.

Fuel, wells and shutoffs

Most standby installs in Nevada County run on propane, because much of the county has no natural gas service. If a home already has a propane tank, the generator can tie into it. If not, a tank set becomes part of the quote. Well pump startup draw is one of the largest loads in a foothill home and is checked before the unit is sized.

Nevada County is in PG&E public safety power shutoff territory. In the October 2026 shutoff scope, about 2,137 PG&E customers in the county were in line to lose power, and on November 18 outages peaked at 5,019 customers out. 30.4% of the county is 65 or older. Those figures describe the county, not a single Nevada City outage.

County age and PG&E shutoff scope figures are from reporting carried in this project’s November 2026 research. They are context for why demand exists here, not a promise about any single outage.

Sizing, in brief

Essentials only, about 10–14 kW. Most of the house, about 18–22 kW. Whole house with AC and a well pump, about 22–26 kW. Final size depends on the home’s loads and is confirmed at the in-home estimate. Generac is the brand most often installed locally; Kohler and Cummins units are also installed and serviced in this market.
